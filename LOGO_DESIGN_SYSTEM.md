## 2. Master Vector Glyphs & Construction

### A. The Wisible Master Mark ("The Optical Prism W")
- **Concept**: A monolithic geometric **'W'** formed by two intersecting refractive prisms, symbolizing clarity, foresight, and high-visibility technology (*"Wisdom + Visible"*).
- **Stroke Geometry**: 2.5px uniform monoline with 45° faceted terminal cuts.
- **Colorway**: Pure Zenith White (`#FCFAF7`) with an ambient Superhuman Magenta gradient refraction (`#F6AADD` → `#792D4B`).

### B. The UP Mark ("The Aerodynamic Ascension")
- **Concept**: An upward aerodynamic vector arrow (`↑`) emerging seamlessly through the negative space of a minimalist geometric monogram **"UP"**.
- **Visual Feeling**: Vertical momentum, zero drag, compounding acceleration.
- **Lockup**: `UP` + `by Wisible` in monospaced micro-caps with `letter-spacing: 0.18em`.

### C. The Genius Goat Mark ("The Synaptic Horns")
- **Concept**: Minimalist faceted geometric ram profile where the horns double as dual forward-pointing lightning vectors and neural synapse nodes.
- **Visual Feeling**: Unrivaled IQ, tactical wit, high-speed computation.
- **Lockup**: `GENIUS GOAT` + `by Wisible`.

## 3. Clear Space & Scalability Rules

- **Clear Space Rule**: A mandatory perimeter of **2X** (where X = the apex height of the central vector node) must be maintained around all logo lockups on all applications.
- **Minimum Digital Sizes**:
  - **App Icon / Favicon**: `32px × 32px` (Squircle with 22% border radius, 1px internal Mulberry glow).
  - **Header Nav Lockup**: `24px` glyph height + `14px` wordmark + `9px` "by Wisible" endorsement badge.
  - **Hero / Billboard**: `120px+` glyph height with fine internal refractive lines.

## 4. Logo Color Variations & Contexts

1. **Obsidian Dark (Primary Dark Mode)**:
   - Glyph Fill / Stroke: 3-Stop Jewel Gradient (`#8B5CF6 → #EC4899 → #F97316`)
   - Wordmark: Zenith White (`#FFFFFF`)
   - Background: Warm Obsidian (`#141413`) or Deep Obsidian Surface (`#181622`)

2. **Luxe Light (Day Mode)**:
   - Glyph: 3-Stop Jewel Gradient or Solid Matte Berry (`#9D174D`)
   - Wordmark: Ink Display (`#26292C`)
   - Background: 300gsm Cotton Paper Wash (`#F7F6F4`) or Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`)

3. **Monochrome Solid**:
   - Glyph & Wordmark: Solid Ink Slate (`#26292C`) or Solid White (`#FFFFFF`)
