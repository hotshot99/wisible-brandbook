07 / Brand Governance

Design Do's and Don'ts

Rules for preserving the authentic luxury and speed of the Superhuman design language.

✓ ALWAYS DO
  • • Always use warm Obsidian (#141413) as the canvas base.
  • • Always use Mulberry (#792D4B) & Electric Magenta (#F6AADD).
  • • Always include the "by Wisible" endorsement on master locks and footers.
  • • Always provide tactile keycaps (⌘K, Q, ↑) on all actions.
✕ NEVER DO
  • • Never use generic corporate blues or cold gray borders.
  • • Never alter the 2X clear space ratio around the vector marks.
  • • Never clutter the screen with cartoon illustrations or heavy drop shadows.
  • • Never use generic corporate buzzwords.
