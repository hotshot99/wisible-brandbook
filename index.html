07 / Brand Governance

The Jewel Rule & Governance

✓ ALWAYS DO
  • • 100% Color Identity: Wisible and UP share the exact same color tokens across all platforms.
  • • Use Solid Matte Berry (#9D174D) as the universal primary CTA button on Web, App & Mobile.
  • • Apply the 3-stop Master Gradient (#8B5CF6 → #EC4899 → #F97316) strictly to Logo Glyphs & Active Sparks (≤5% area).
  • • Keep page wash on #F7F6F4 (Day) or #141413 (Obsidian Void).
  • • Render large financial and northstar metrics in pure Ink (#26292C).
  • • Pair all key actions with physical keycap hints (⌘K, G, Enter).
✕ NEVER DO
  • • Never create separate or divergent color palettes between Wisible and UP.
  • • Never apply gradient fills to primary CTA button bodies (Solid #9D174D only).
  • • Never apply gradient fills to full cards, tables, or background panels.
  • • Never use generic corporate blue (#0066FF) or cold gray (#E2E8F0).
  • • Never distort the 45° angle cuts on vector monograms.
  • • Never compromise the 1X clear space perimeter around the vector marks.
